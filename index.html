Research

Beware of Agentic Botnets: Scalable Untargeted Promptware Attacks via Universal and Transferable Adversarial HalluSquatting

Aya Spira, Stav Cohen, Elad Feldman, Ron Bitton, Avishai Wool, Ben Nassi
arXiv preprint, 2026

LLMs and coding agents hallucinate resource identifiers, and many of those hallucinations are predictable. We studied them across models and production coding agents, and showed how an attacker can use them for AI supply-chain attacks.
